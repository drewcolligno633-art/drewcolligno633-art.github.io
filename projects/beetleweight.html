3 lb beetleweight combat robot

A 3 lb beetleweight combat robot I designed and built. It is a two wheel drivetrain with a eggbeater weapon that is vertically symetric.

Design

The robot is a two-wheel drive with a vertical eggbeater spinner. The design focuses on the vertical symetry of the entrie robot, this allows for the robot to drive even when flipped upside down. The eggbeater weapon make the most out of the weapon weight be concentrating the mass at the outer edge.

Build

The entire robot except for the weapon and fasteners was 3D printed out of carbon fiber nylon and TPU 95A. The drivetrain was built using standard RC car components and powered by two 400Rpm brushed motors. The weapon was powered by a brushless 3600rpm motor.

How it performed

The design performed as intended and was able to sucessfully take advantage of the symetric drive. The weapon performed well in testing.

What I'd change

If I were to design a new version I would swap the drivetrains wheels to have more traction as the 3d printed wheels on the current design slipped alot on the wood floor of the battlebox. I would also respec the drive and weapon motors to try and get more power out of the weapon and scale down the overkill power of the drivetrain
